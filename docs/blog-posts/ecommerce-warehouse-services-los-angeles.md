## FAQs

### Can I use a warehouse if my online store takes preorders?

Yes, if the provider supports holding orders until the agreed release date.

For a preorder launch, confirm whether orders can enter the system before products arrive and what will prevent early dispatch. Set a release rule for each affected item, especially when one purchase includes both available goods and preorder products. Ask how customers will receive updates if supplier dates change. Westfield Prep Center can review your launch requirements before you commit to a plan.

### Is outsourcing worthwhile if my store only gets a few orders each day?

It can be worthwhile when the full cost fits your order volume.

Compare the proposed monthly bill with the time your team spends fulfilling orders and the expense of operating your own space. Check whether the provider has a monthly minimum, since that can affect smaller stores. Include quiet months in your comparison. Outsourcing may free staff for sales and customer support, but a low order count alone doesn't show whether the arrangement makes sense.

### How much would warehousing cost for a store with changing monthly sales?

The cost depends on stored volume, order activity, and the provider's charging rules.

Request estimates for a quiet month, a normal month, and your busiest expected month using the same product data. Include receiving, storage, order handling, packing materials, and shipping in the comparison. Ask which charges apply even when no orders ship. A useful quote states its assumptions, so you can see which parts of the bill may change as your sales rise or fall.

### How do I choose a Los Angeles warehouse before signing a contract?

Choose a provider after confirming it can handle your specific products.

Ask for a walkthrough of an order that resembles yours, including any unusual requirements. Review the proposed contract length, cancellation terms, and responsibility for lost or damaged goods. Request references from businesses with a similar product range where available. For location, check the actual operating address and service coverage. Westfield Prep Center can discuss your requirements so you can assess the proposed fit.

### Can a traditional bulk warehouse handle orders containing just one product?

Only if it also offers the individual order services your online store needs.

A facility built around moving pallets may not accept individual customer orders under its standard agreement. Ask whether the quoted service covers single-unit orders and direct delivery to shoppers. Check whether another company would perform those tasks and charge separately. Compare the full scope against your sales model before choosing a provider, since storage capacity alone does not show whether the facility suits ecommerce.

### What should happen to warehouse inventory when a customer cancels an order?

The item should return to available stock only after the cancellation is confirmed.

The process depends on whether the order is still waiting, already being packed, or has left the facility. Ask how the provider stops eligible orders and updates the stock count afterward. Confirm what happens when your store accepts a cancellation too late to stop shipping. Test this situation during setup so your customer service team knows when it can safely promise a refund.

### Are customer returns automatically included in a warehouse agreement?

No, return handling needs to be included explicitly in the agreed service scope.

Ask whether the provider can receive returned parcels, record their condition, and wait for your instructions before putting items back on sale. Define what counts as suitable for resale and who decides when an item cannot be sold again. Confirm any separate inspection or handling fees. These details matter when shoppers return opened products, incomplete sets, or goods without clear order information attached.

### How can I lower storage costs when some products stop selling?

Review slow sellers and compare removal costs with the cost of keeping them.

For ecommerce storage solutions in Los Angeles, ask how long stored goods can remain before additional fees apply. Consider whether a discount, supplier return, or removal makes financial sense for each affected product. Confirm any fees for pulling stock out before deciding. Review these items regularly, using sales history and expected demand, so you avoid paying month after month for goods with no realistic sales plan.

### Can subscription orders include a different printed insert each month?

Yes, if the provider accepts insert changes and can apply the correct version.

Agree on how each month's insert will be identified and when the previous version must stop being used. Check what happens to leftover printed materials and whether the insert adds a handling charge. Send instructions before the next subscription batch begins. A sample order can help confirm the right insert reaches the right subscription group before the rest of that month's orders are released.

### Can I move to a new warehouse while my store keeps taking orders?

Yes, if both providers and your store follow a planned transfer schedule.

Decide which location will handle new orders during the move and when the old location will stop accepting work. Reconcile the transferred quantities before making that stock available for sale again. Keep enough goods available to cover orders during the change where possible. Confirm how outstanding orders and tracking records will be handled, so customers are not left waiting while responsibility moves between providers.
