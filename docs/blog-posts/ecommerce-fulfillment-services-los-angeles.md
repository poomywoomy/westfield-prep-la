## FAQs

### How much will fulfillment cost for my Los Angeles store?

Your total depends on stock volume, order size, storage needs, and the services included in your agreement.

Ask Westfield Prep Center for a written quote based on a typical month of sales. Request separate figures for receiving, storage, item handling, packaging, postage, and any account fees. Include slower months in your comparison because minimum charges may affect your budget. A useful quote should show which charges change as your sales increase or decrease over time.

### How do I choose an Ecommerce Fulfillment Provider Los Angeles businesses can rely on?

Choose a provider whose documented performance and contract terms fit your Los Angeles business's needs.

Request references from sellers with a similar sales volume, then ask how performance is measured and reported. Review liability terms, insurance requirements, and any limits on compensation. If a trial is available, define success before it starts. Comparing evidence from actual accounts gives you a stronger basis for choosing than relying on broad promises about service quality alone.

### Would outsourcing help if packing orders takes up most of my week?

Outsourcing can free your time for sales, product development, and customer support when warehouse tasks consume your working week.

Start by recording how many hours you spend managing fulfillment each month. Compare the value of that time with the proposed service charges. You will still need someone to oversee the relationship and review results. The benefit is strongest when the hours released support work your business currently delays because you are occupied with daily operations.

### Should a small Los Angeles store outsource or keep fulfillment in-house?

Keep fulfillment in-house if your existing setup meets demand at a lower total cost and manageable workload.

Compare rent, staff time, equipment, software, and supplies against an outside provider's quote. Include the owner's unpaid working hours in that calculation. Outsourcing may become more practical when expansion requires another lease or additional employees. Review both options using the same monthly sales figures so the decision reflects your business rather than a general order volume rule.

### How does fulfillment work during the first month after changing providers?

The first month should follow an agreed transition plan with clear responsibility for orders placed during the change.

Decide when the previous provider stops accepting new work and when the new arrangement begins. Reserve time to reconcile stock records between both locations. Keep a dated list of purchases that remain with the previous provider. A short review period helps your Los Angeles business spot transition problems before closing the old account or ending access to its records.

### How can I reduce delivery charges for orders leaving Los Angeles?

Compare carrier services against customer destinations and delivery needs before choosing the lowest advertised rate.

Review recent invoices for residential charges, remote-area fees, fuel surcharges, and address corrections. Group your findings by destination to see where spending is concentrated. Customers may accept a slower service when the delivery estimate is clear before payment. Ask which rates are available under your agreement, and compare the final billed amount rather than the starting price shown online.

### What happens when a customer sends back an opened product?

Check an opened return against your written return rules before anyone decides whether it can be sold again.

Define acceptable condition, missing parts, signs of use, and any evidence you need recorded. Ask Westfield Prep Center about available return handling before directing customers to send items back. Keep refund approval separate from the physical inspection unless your agreement says otherwise. This helps prevent a returned item from being treated as saleable without your approval.

### What should I do if my Shopify connection stops sending orders?

Pause any manual imports until you confirm which purchases have already reached the fulfillment system.

A connection failure can create duplicate shipments if the same purchase arrives again after service resumes. Record affected order numbers and agree how they will be checked before release. Confirm connection support with your provider before launch, including who investigates an outage. Once access returns, reconcile the missing purchases against completed shipments before allowing normal updates to continue for your store.

### Can I sell a preorder alongside items already available?

Yes, but decide whether mixed purchases will wait for the preorder or ship in separate parts.

Show that policy before checkout so buyers understand when to expect each item. Confirm whether your provider supports holding purchases or releasing only available products. Separate shipments may create additional handling and postage charges. If an arrival date changes, update affected customers promptly and explain their options. Keep preorder quantities distinguishable from goods already available for sale in your store.

### Who handles a parcel marked delivered when the customer cannot find it?

Your business should own the customer response while the responsible party investigates the carrier's delivery record.

Ask the buyer to check the delivery location, household members, and building reception where relevant. Then review the tracking details and request delivery evidence if available. Your agreement should identify who submits carrier claims and what records are required. Set a clear internal deadline for deciding on a replacement or refund, so the customer receives an answer while the investigation continues.
