## FAQs

### What is multichannel fulfillment if I already sell through several online stores?

Multichannel fulfillment means using one fulfillment operation to serve orders from more than one sales channel. Your website and marketplace listings remain separate places where customers buy, but their purchases can draw on a shared product supply. The term describes the fulfillment arrangement, rather than the stores themselves. For a Los Angeles seller, this can mean using one local provider instead of arranging a separate warehouse service for every place the business sells. 

### How does multichannel fulfillment work when my stores use different product codes?

Each store’s product code needs to connect to the correct warehouse product record. Otherwise, two listings for the same item may look like different products, or similar products may be confused. Before starting, prepare a reference showing which store codes represent each physical item. Ask Westfield Prep Center how they’ll check those references during setup. Test purchases from each channel can reveal mismatches before regular customer orders enter the agreed fulfillment process.

### How much does multichannel fulfillment cost for a Los Angeles business?

The cost depends on your stock, order volume, product sizes, and the work requested. A useful estimate should separate receiving, storage, order handling, packaging materials, and shipping rather than present one unexplained total. Share a typical month of orders and the dimensions of your products when requesting a quote. Also ask about minimum monthly charges and work outside the standard service. No reliable price can be stated without first reviewing those details for your business.

### How do I choose a multichannel fulfillment provider if my products have special storage needs?

Choose a provider that can confirm suitable storage conditions for your specific products. Explain any temperature limits, moisture concerns, shelf life, or restrictions before discussing a start date. Ask what the facility can support and request written confirmation of any essential conditions. A general promise to handle e-commerce orders does not establish suitability for every product. If you can’t confirm your requirements, keep those goods out of the proposed arrangement until you have clear answers.

### Can I keep some products at my own Los Angeles location?

Yes, a business can use outside fulfillment while keeping selected products at its own location. The important step is deciding which location handles each purchase before an order is released. For multichannel pick and pack, ask how orders containing products from both locations would be divided and tracked. Customers may receive separate parcels, so their order messages should explain that possibility. Confirm the arrangement before offering products together across your website and other sales channels.

### Should I send preorder purchases for fulfillment before the stock arrives?

Preorder purchases should remain on hold until the required stock is available for release. A preorder records customer demand, but it does not mean the warehouse has goods ready to send. Agree how those purchases will be identified and who authorizes release after stock arrives. Keep the promised release date separate from an estimated delivery date. If the supply date changes, update customers through the sales channel where they placed their original order.

### Can recurring subscription purchases use the same fulfillment arrangement?

Yes, recurring purchases can use the same arrangement if each cycle creates a clear order. A subscription agreement alone should not be treated as an instruction to send another parcel. Confirm how payment status, skipped cycles, and cancellations affect the orders released for fulfillment. Westfield Prep Center should review the proposed process with you before recurring purchases begin. This helps distinguish a valid renewal from a customer account that should receive nothing during that cycle.

### What happens if my online store stops sending orders?

Orders that never reach the fulfillment provider cannot enter the normal handling process. First, identify the last order received successfully and compare it with the store’s order list. When arranging Multichannel Order Fulfillment Services, agree how missing purchases will be reported and whether an approved backup submission method is available. Check every affected order before resending anything. Otherwise, a restored connection could send the same purchase again and create a risk of duplicate customer shipments.

### Can a customer change an address after placing an order?

An address change may be possible before the parcel reaches the carrier. The available options depend on how far the order has progressed and the carrier’s rules. Send the request through the agreed contact method and wait for confirmation before telling the customer it is complete. Once a parcel is moving, a delivery change may be restricted or carry a charge. Update the original store record too, so future purchases use the customer’s intended address.

### Is using a Los Angeles fulfillment provider the same as Amazon FBA?

No, using an independent fulfillment provider is a different arrangement from enrolling products in Amazon FBA. Amazon FBA uses Amazon’s fulfillment network under its program requirements. An independent provider works under a separate service agreement and may handle orders from several stores. If you sell on Amazon, confirm how each order will be fulfilled and what your listings will tell buyers. Do not assume that using a local warehouse automatically gives your products Prime eligibility.
