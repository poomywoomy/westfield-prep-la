## FAQs

### 1. How do I outsource Shopify order fulfillment when I currently ship from home?

Start by choosing a provider, agreeing on terms, and testing the store connection before transferring live orders.

For a Los Angeles business moving away from home fulfillment, set a switch date and decide which orders you'll keep. Send a small stock shipment first if the provider allows it. Westfield Prep Center can discuss your starting requirements so you know what information is needed before handing over daily work to the warehouse team.

### 2. How much does Shopify 3PL fulfillment cost for a small Los Angeles store?

The cost depends on your products, order volume, storage needs, and the work each order requires.

Request an itemized quote for Shopify 3PL pricing rather than relying on a single advertised rate. Include receiving, storage, packing materials, postage, account fees, and any monthly minimum. Give Westfield Prep Center sample orders with actual product weights and sizes. That makes it easier to compare the proposed charges against what your Los Angeles store would pay.

### 3. What are the benefits of using a 3PL if I cannot hire warehouse staff?

A 3PL can reduce the need to hire warehouse staff or manage your own storage space.

This matters when a Los Angeles store owner spends evenings handling parcels or arranging cover for absent employees. Compare the provider's total fee with your current rent, equipment, wages, and management time. Outsourcing does not remove your responsibility for customer service, but it can move much of the physical warehouse workload outside your business.

### 4. How do I choose a Shopify fulfillment provider for products with special storage needs?

Choose a provider that can demonstrate relevant experience and explain its service terms in writing.

If you sell products with expiry dates or special storage needs, ask whether the facility can handle those requirements. Request references from stores with similar products and order volumes. Review insurance coverage, contract limits, and how performance is measured. A low quoted rate is less useful when the provider cannot meet the needs of your Los Angeles business.

### 5. How does Shopify inventory sync with a 3PL if I hold stock in two places?

Inventory usually syncs through an app or connection that links warehouse records with your Shopify locations.

Each product needs a matching stock code so updates reach the correct listing. If your Los Angeles store also holds stock elsewhere, confirm which location each update changes. Ask how often updates run and what happens when the connection fails. Test a stock adjustment before launch, then confirm that the change appears correctly in your store. 

### 6. Can a 3PL automatically fulfill Shopify orders while holding purchases that need review?

Yes, a connected 3PL can receive and process eligible Shopify orders automatically when its system supports that workflow.

Your settings determine which purchases are released and which need review. For example, you may want to hold back unpaid orders or purchases flagged for possible fraud. Confirm how those rules pass to the provider before enabling automation. Run test orders with different payment states so you can see which requests reach the warehouse and which remain held. 

### 7. What happens if a customer changes their address after an order reaches the warehouse?

An address change may be possible before the parcel is dispatched, but it depends on the order's progress.

Contact the provider as soon as the customer reports the mistake. Updating Shopify alone may not update a request already sent to the warehouse. Ask whether the order can be stopped, whether a new label is needed, and what charges apply. Once the carrier has it, available options depend on that carrier's rules and the shipment's current status. 

### 8. Can my Los Angeles Shopify store use a 3PL to send orders overseas?

International fulfillment may be possible if the provider supports the destination and your products meet its requirements.

Before offering overseas delivery from Los Angeles, confirm who supplies customs details, product values, and country-of-origin information. Decide how customers will pay any duties and taxes. Certain goods face restrictions that differ by country. Ask the provider to review your product range and intended markets before you publish international shipping options in your store. 

### 9. Can a 3PL handle monthly Shopify subscription orders without sending future deliveries early?

Yes, subscription orders can go to a 3PL if its connection supports your subscription setup and release schedule.

Check prepaid plans separately from orders charged at each renewal. A prepaid order can include deliveries scheduled for future dates, so the warehouse must receive the correct release instructions. Test a subscription through more than one delivery cycle. This helps confirm that future shipments are not sent early or overlooked after the first parcel leaves Los Angeles. 

### 10. Can I keep local pickup orders separate from orders sent to my 3PL?

Yes, pickup orders can stay separate when your Shopify locations and order rules are configured correctly.

A Los Angeles store offering collection should confirm that the warehouse connection checks the delivery method before accepting a shipping request. Otherwise, a purchase intended for pickup could be sent out as a parcel. Test one collection order and one shipped order before launch. Make sure each reaches the right location and triggers the appropriate customer notification.
