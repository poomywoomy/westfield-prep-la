### How can I improve accuracy when several products look almost identical?

Clear SKU management helps staff tell similar products apart before the wrong variant reaches a customer.

Give every size, color, and pack quantity its own product code and barcode. Use matching names in your store and warehouse records. Keep product photos current and remove old codes from active listings. Ask your provider how scans are checked against each order and how unclear records are flagged before picking starts, especially when adding new variants to an existing range.
